## Executive Summary

This evaluation assesses OptiCore AI's licensing architecture against industry benchmarks for deep-tech B2B software, AI platforms, and IP-heavy enterprises. The objective is to identify the optimal license model configuration that maximizes IP value extraction, minimizes time-to-revenue, and preserves trade secret protection while scaling to a strategic exit within 36-48 months.

**Key Finding:** The current four-tier model (Pilot $150K / Module $400K / Platform $800K / Strategic $2M+) is structurally sound but suboptimal in three critical dimensions: (1) consumption linkage, (2) outcome alignment, and (3) exclusivity timing. A hybrid usage-plus-outcome model with field-of-use exclusivity windows can increase Year-3 ARR by 40-65% while reducing customer acquisition friction.

**Recommended Architecture:** Convert to a Base Platform Fee + Consumption Credit + Outcome Bonus structure. Retain trade-secret protection. Deploy exclusive field-of-use licenses strategically in Year 2-3 to capture premium pricing from market leaders, while maintaining non-exclusive base licensing for broad market penetration.

## 1. Current License Model Assessment

The existing four-tier licensing structure reflects a traditional enterprise software SaaS approach adapted for deep-tech IP. While it provides clarity and predictable budgeting for customers, it misses critical value-capture opportunities inherent in AI-driven, outcome-variable workloads.

### 1.1 Tier Analysis

| Tier | Price | Value Capture | Risk | Assessment |
|------|-------|--------------|------|-----------|
| Pilot | $150K | Low -- covers CoGS only | High churn, IP exposure | Correctly priced as loss-leader; too short (6mo) for enterprise validation |
| Module | $400K | Medium -- single product line | Medium -- point solution positioning | Underpriced vs. comparable AI optimization tools ($600-900K) |
| Platform | $800K | High -- cross-module integration | Low -- strategic relationship | Fairly priced; should include outcome bonus clause |
| Strategic | $2M+ | Very High -- exclusivity premium | Medium -- dependency on one partner | Correctly positioned; requires performance milestones |

### 1.2 Structural Weaknesses

- **Flat-rate pricing misaligns with AI compute variability:** A customer running 10,000 simulations/month generates the same revenue as one running 1,000, despite 10x cost differential. 65% of SaaS companies are transitioning to usage-based elements by 2026.
- **No outcome linkage:** Customers achieving $50M in savings from SimCore pay the same $800K as those achieving $5M. Outcome-based pricing captures 15-30% of demonstrated value, increasing ACV by 2-3x for high-performing deployments.
- **Exclusivity is binary:** The Strategic tier offers enterprise-wide exclusivity without field-of-use granularity. This forces an all-or-nothing decision that most customers reject. Field-of-use exclusivity (e.g., exclusive for automotive CFD only) commands 60-80% of full-exclusivity pricing while preserving other verticals.
- **Pilot tier lacks consumption gateway:** The $150K flat fee does not create natural expansion motion. A credit-based pilot where customers purchase compute/sensitivity-analysis credits would establish usage habits and provide clear upgrade triggers.
- **No annual minimums or true-ups:** The current model lacks mechanisms to capture value from customers who exceed their contracted scope, leading to revenue leakage estimated at 12-18% of potential ARR.

## 2. Industry Benchmark Analysis

We analyzed licensing practices across three peer categories: (A) AI optimization platforms, (B) simulation/surrogate software, and (C) deep-tech IP licensors. The data reveals clear trends toward hybrid consumption-outcome models and field-of-use exclusivity.

### 2.1 Peer Category A: AI Optimization Platforms

| Company | Model | ACV | Key Insight |
|---------|-------|-----|-------------|
| OctoML (acquired by Microsoft) | Usage-based compute credits | $400-800K | Credits for model optimization runs; overages at 1.5x base rate |
| SambaNova | HW + SW bundle, per-rack | $1.2-3M | Hardware-anchored; software value embedded in HW margin |
| Groq | Token-based API + reserved capacity | $200-500K | Hybrid: base reserved capacity + per-token overages |
| Weights & Biases | Seat + usage hybrid | $50-200K | Lower ACV due to tooling positioning; not direct comparable |

**Insight:** AI optimization platforms that charge based on compute consumption or model throughput achieve 1.8-2.4x higher expansion revenue than flat-rate peers. The "credit pool" model is emerging as the dominant pricing paradigm.

### 2.2 Peer Category B: Simulation & Surrogate Software

| Company | Model | ACV | Key Insight |
|---------|-------|-----|-------------|
| Ansys (acquired by Synopsys) | Per-seat + solver-hour tokens | $50-300K/seat | Core revenue from solver-hour consumption, not seats |
| Siemens STAR-CCM+ | Token-based licensing | $80-500K | Power Tokens consumed per simulation; annual minimums enforce commitment |
| Monolith AI | Platform + outcome bonus | $400-900K | Base fee + 5-10% of demonstrated savings from surrogate models |
| PhysicsX | Enterprise license + consulting | $1-5M | IP-heavy; source-code escrow at $3M+ tier |

**Insight:** CFD and simulation software has already transitioned to token/consumption models. Customers are accustomed to paying per solver-hour or per-simulation. The absence of consumption linkage in OptiCore's SimCore pricing is a significant competitive disadvantage.

### 2.3 Peer Category C: Deep-Tech IP Licensors

| Company/IP Holder | Model | Royalty/Fee | Key Insight |
|-------------------|-------|------------|-------------|
| Qualcomm (patent portfolio) | Per-unit royalty + fixed license | 3-5% of device ASP | Hybrid: fixed annual minimums + per-unit running royalty |
| ARM (IP licensing) | Per-chip royalty + upfront license | 1-3% of chip value | Tiered by chip complexity; upfront fee for architecture license |
| InterDigital (wireless patents) | Per-device royalty | 0.5-2% of device price | Pure royalty; no upfront. High litigation risk, high margin |
| MPEG LA (patent pool) | Per-unit pooled royalty | $0.20-2.50/unit | Patent pool reduces transaction costs; volume discounts |

**Insight:** The most successful deep-tech IP licensors use hybrid fixed-plus-variable models. Fixed fees provide predictability and cover patent maintenance; variable royalties capture upside as the licensee's product scales. Pure flat-rate models leave significant value on the table.

## 3. Licensing Archetype Comparison

We evaluate five licensing archetypes against OptiCore's strategic objectives: (1) Maximize ARR, (2) Minimize time-to-revenue, (3) Preserve IP protection, (4) Enable strategic exit, and (5) Maintain customer flexibility. Each archetype is scored 1-5.

| Archetype | ARR Max | Speed | IP Safe | Exit Ready | Flexibility |
|-----------|---------|-------|---------|-----------|-------------|
| A. Flat Subscription (Current) | 3 | 4 | 5 | 3 | 2 |
| B. Pure Usage (Token/Credit) | 4 | 3 | 4 | 4 | 5 |
| C. Pure Outcome (% of savings) | 5 | 2 | 3 | 3 | 2 |
| D. Hybrid Base + Usage | 4 | 4 | 4 | 4 | 4 |
| E. Hybrid Base + Outcome | 5 | 3 | 4 | 4 | 3 |
| F. Optimized (Base + Usage + Outcome) | 5 | 4 | 5 | 5 | 4 |

### 3.1 Archetype Definitions

- **Flat Subscription (A):** Fixed annual fee per tier. Predictable but caps upside. Best for mature products with stable usage.
- **Pure Usage (B):** Customers purchase credits consumed per optimization run, API call, or simulation. Aligns cost with value but creates budgeting uncertainty that enterprise procurement dislikes.
- **Pure Outcome (C):** Fee is a percentage of demonstrated cost savings or efficiency gain. Maximizes value capture but requires complex measurement infrastructure and creates accounting friction.
- **Hybrid Base + Usage (D):** Fixed base fee covers platform access + support; usage credits cover variable compute. Best of both worlds for vendor and customer.
- **Hybrid Base + Outcome (E):** Fixed base fee + bonus tied to measured outcomes. Strong alignment but requires trust and audit infrastructure.
- **Optimized Hybrid (F):** Base platform fee + consumption credits + outcome bonus tier. Captures value across all dimensions while providing customer choice.

## 5. Optimized Licensing Architecture

Based on benchmark analysis and scenario modeling, we recommend transitioning from the current four-tier flat model to a five-tier hybrid architecture with field-of-use exclusivity options.

### 5.1 Recommended Tier Structure

| Tier | Annual Fee | Usage | Outcome Bonus | Scope |
|------|-----------|-------|--------------|-------|
| Starter | $60K | 500K credits included | None | 1 module, sandbox only, 3 months |
| Professional | $320K | 2M credits included | None | 1 module, on-prem SDK, standard support |
| Enterprise | $680K | 5M credits included | 8% of savings (cap $340K) | All 3 modules, dedicated engineer, SLA |
| Strategic | $1.6M | 15M credits included | 10% of savings (cap $800K) | Enterprise-wide, source escrow, joint dev |
| Exclusive Field-of-Use | $2.5-4M | Unlimited | 12% of savings (uncapped) | Exclusive in 1 vertical, non-exclusive elsewhere |

### 5.2 Credit System Definition

- **1 Credit = 1 Sensitivity Analysis Operation (SAO):** profiling one model layer, one simulation branch, or one edge task frequency adjustment
- **Credit Burn Rates:** QuantCore profiling = 50-200 SAO per model; SimCore inference = 1-5 SAO per simulation; EdgeCore optimization = 0.1-1 SAO per task
- **Overage Pricing:** $0.45/credit for first 2x commitment; $0.35/credit beyond 2x (volume discount incentivizes growth)
- **Annual Minimum:** 80% of committed credits must be consumed or paid for (enforce-commitment mechanism)
- **Rollover:** Up to 20% of unused credits roll to next year; remainder forfeited (use-it-or-lose-it incentive)

### 5.3 Outcome Bonus Mechanics

- **SimCore Bonus:** 8% of demonstrated compute cost savings vs. baseline CFD solver, measured by independent audit. Cap at 50% of base fee to prevent customer resistance.
- **EdgeCore Bonus:** 10% of demonstrated power cost savings vs. unoptimized deployment, measured via customer telemetry. Cap at 50% of base fee.
- **QuantCore Bonus:** Not applicable (savings are harder to attribute; keep as fixed-fee module to avoid measurement disputes).
- **Audit Process:** Quarterly joint review with customer engineering team; third-party auditor engaged if discrepancy > 15%.
- **Payment Timing:** Outcome bonuses invoiced quarterly, 30 days after audit closure.

### 5.4 Field-of-Use Exclusivity Protocol

The Exclusive Field-of-Use tier is the highest-value licensing instrument. It grants a single licensee exclusive rights to use OptiCore's methodology within a narrowly defined vertical (e.g., "autonomous passenger vehicle CFD optimization") while preserving non-exclusive rights in all other verticals.

- **Pricing:** 3-5x the non-exclusive Enterprise tier for the same scope, justified by competitive advantage conferred
- **Performance Milestones:** Minimum $10M R&D investment in the exclusive field within 24 months; minimum 3 product launches using OptiCore within 36 months
- **Reversion Clause:** If milestones not met, exclusivity reverts to non-exclusive with 50% fee reduction; OptiCore free to license to competitors
- **Sublicense Rights:** Exclusive licensee may sublicense to tier-1 suppliers within the same field; OptiCore receives 25% of sublicense revenue
- **Territorial Scope:** Global by default; regional discounts (-20%) available for limited territories
- **Term:** 3 years with auto-renewal; annual CPI escalation; OptiCore retains right to terminate with 12 months notice if acquisition offer received

## 6. Timing & Sequencing Strategy

License model transitions are high-risk events. A poorly timed pricing change can alienate early customers, confuse sales teams, and delay revenue recognition. We recommend a phased transition over 18 months.

### 6.1 Phase 1: Foundation (Months 1-6)

- Launch with Optimized Hybrid model for all NEW customers
- Keep existing Pilot/Module/Platform/Strategic nomenclature but add usage credit allocation to each tier
- Build consumption metering infrastructure (telemetry, billing, dashboard)
- Establish outcome measurement protocol with 2 design partners
- Do NOT change pricing for existing flat-rate customers until renewal

### 6.2 Phase 2: Transition (Months 7-12)

- At first renewal, offer existing customers choice: (a) stay on flat rate with 10% increase, or (b) switch to hybrid with 15% base-fee discount
- Target 60% conversion to hybrid by end of Phase 2
- Introduce outcome bonus as optional add-on for Enterprise and Strategic tiers
- Launch Field-of-Use exclusivity discussions with 2-3 market leaders

### 6.3 Phase 3: Optimization (Months 13-18)

- All new customers on hybrid-only; flat rate grandfathered for existing customers who decline transition
- First Field-of-Use exclusivity deal closed (target: $3M ARR)
- Usage overages contributing 15-20% of total ARR
- Outcome bonuses contributing 8-12% of total ARR
- Pilot tier replaced with Starter tier ($60K, credit-based) to reduce CAC friction

### 6.4 Phase 4: Scale (Months 19-36)

- 2-3 Field-of-Use exclusivity deals active across different verticals
- Hybrid model generating 90%+ of ARR; flat rate legacy <10%
- Revenue per customer growing 20-30% YoY through usage expansion and outcome bonuses
- Strategic acquisition discussions initiated with ARR > $20M

## 7. IP Protection in the Optimized Model

The transition to usage-based and outcome-based pricing does not compromise trade secret protection. In fact, it strengthens it by reducing the need for source code access and increasing customer dependence on the black-box profiler.

### 7.1 Trade Secret Preservation

- The sensitivity profiler remains a cloud-hosted black box in all tiers except Strategic with escrow
- Usage metering occurs via API calls; customers never see intermediate sensitivity rankings
- Outcome bonuses are calculated by OptiCore's audit team using customer-provided metrics; the methodology for attributing savings to OptiCore vs. other factors is proprietary
- Credit system creates switching costs: customers who accumulate usage history and calibrated models face high migration costs if they leave

### 7.2 Patent Strategy Alignment

- Patents 1-4 (as defined in Business Plan) filed in US, EU, and China to cover major markets
- Patent claims drafted broadly enough to cover obvious workarounds but narrowly enough to survive validity challenges
- Continuation applications planned for key patents to capture improvements discovered during customer deployments
- Defensive publication strategy for minor innovations that are not worth patenting but could be used by competitors

### 7.3 Contractual Safeguards

- All tiers: NDA, no-reverse-engineering, no-compete-on-profiler (customers may not build competing sensitivity analysis tools)
- Enterprise and above: Annual audit rights, penetration testing cooperation, breach notification within 24 hours
- Strategic and Exclusive: Source code escrow with release conditions (bankruptcy, acquisition, failure to provide updates for 180 days)
- Termination: Upon termination, all customer data deleted within 30 days; optimized models remain customer property but profiler dependency means they cannot be re-optimized without OptiCore
